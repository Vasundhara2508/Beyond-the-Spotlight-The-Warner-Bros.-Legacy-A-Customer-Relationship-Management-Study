#  CRM Analytics & Churn Prediction in the Streaming Industry

### Warner Bros vs Netflix – Customer Relationship Management Study

## Project Overview

The global streaming industry has become highly competitive, where **customer retention and churn management** play a critical role in long-term business success.

This project analyzes **Customer Relationship Management (CRM) strategies** in the streaming ecosystem by comparing **Warner Bros Discovery and Netflix**.

The study integrates **movie franchise performance analysis, machine learning churn prediction, and consumer perception surveys** to identify the factors influencing **viewer engagement and subscription retention**.

The goal is to understand why **Netflix maintains lower churn rates despite Warner Bros having strong content franchises**, and how CRM strategies can improve customer loyalty.

---

#  Objectives

1. Analyze **Warner Bros franchise strength and audience demand** using movie performance data.
2. Identify **customer churn drivers** using predictive machine learning models.
3. Evaluate **consumer perception of Warner Bros vs Netflix** through survey analytics.
4. Perform a **CRM gap analysis** between the two companies.
5. Develop an **interactive analytics dashboard** for churn insights and decision support.

#  Key Insights

###  Franchise Strength

Warner Bros possesses **strong movie franchises with high global popularity and revenue performance**.

However, **content strength alone does not guarantee subscriber retention**.

---

### Churn Drivers

Machine learning models revealed that customer churn is primarily influenced by:

* Low engagement levels
* Weak personalization
* Payment reliability
* Subscription behavior

Netflix reduces churn through **data-driven CRM systems and recommendation algorithms**.

---

###  Consumer Perception

Survey analysis showed that:

* Warner Bros has strong brand familiarity
* Netflix is perceived as **better at personalization**
* Netflix builds **stronger long-term customer relationships**

#  CRM Gap Analysis
